# TESSA EU AI Act Benchmark Dataset

## Overview

This repository contains the supplementary dataset used to evaluate TESSA, a governance-oriented educational artifact providing guidance on the EU AI Act. TESSA was compared with baseline ChatGPT using 100 benchmark questions across ten thematic evaluation categories.

## Dataset files

* `TESSA_EU_AI_Act_Benchmark_Dataset.xlsx` — TESSA responses and evaluation results.
* `GPT_EU_AI_Act_Benchmark_Dataset.xlsx` — Baseline ChatGPT responses and evaluation results.

The workbooks contain the benchmark questions, expected outcomes, generated responses, and corresponding assessments.

## Evaluation categories

The 100 benchmark questions cover:

1. AI literacy
2. Startup and student projects
3. Risk classification
4. Compliance duties
5. Prohibited AI practices
6. High-risk AI
7. Limited-risk AI
8. Minimal-risk AI
9. General-Purpose AI and foundation models
10. SME perspectives

## Assessment procedure

Responses were initially assessed through an LLM-assisted preliminary review using three alignment classifications: **Yes**, **Partial**, and **No**. An experienced AI regulation lawyer subsequently reviewed the expected outcomes and generated responses, revised the expected outcomes where necessary, and determined the final lawyer-validated classifications.

## Purpose

The dataset supports transparency and reproducibility of the TESSA evaluation. It is provided as supplementary material for the associated research paper.
